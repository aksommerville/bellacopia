Bellacopia Maleficia Guides for GDEX
A few scenarios we can walk players through, to highlight some fun bits, aiming for under 15 minutes each.
Record my time to follow the instructions exactly. Bear in mind a real player will be much less efficient, expect double at least.

Of course there's also Broom Race and Arcade modes from the main menu, also great options for a quickie.

=== End the War ===

1. Get six coins via battle or zoo, and pick up a stick.
2. Buy Divining Rod.
3. Follow the top left root past the Toll Troll into the Battlefield.
4. Deliver the letters.
5. Collect Hookshot.
6. Collect Compass.
7. Strangle Root Devil.

2:28

=== Bridge to Botire ===

1. Get three coins via battle or zoo, and pick up a stick.
2. Pay the Toll Troll, and walk west beyond him.
3. Give the stick to Bridget, and repeat 5 times.

2:29

=== Barrel Hats (Skipping Ahead) ===

1. Enter Cave of Cheating.
2. Take Broom.
3. Fly west and a little north to the knitter's house (southeast Botire), get the first Barrel Hat.
4. Hat all 9 barrels: 2 in Botire, 1 just west, 1 by Magnetic North, 1 by the castle, 1 in Cheapside, 2 in Fractia, 1 in the far northeast corner.
5. Talk to the knitter one last time to get the Bell.
Fun follow-up: Use that Bell to fill all the zoos.

3:18

=== Let the Spirits Drive ===

1. Enter the house by the Cheapside bus stop.
2. Push the chair and go downstairs.
3. Follow signs westward to the Mysterious Treasure. It's a Crystal Ball.
4. Get the Crystal Ball and go back out the same way.
5. Do what the Ball says.

3:27 (to returning to the surface with my Crystal Ball)

=== The Spooky Labyrinth ===

1. Enter Cave of Cheating.
2. Take Bus Stop, Fishpole, Snowglobe. Recommend Potion too, long as you're there.
3. Bus to the Temple.
4. Enter and go north to the monks' cells. Gather statue maze clues (clues and maze are generated randomly per campaign).
5. Southwest thru the statue maze, into the Labyrinth.
6. South and a little east to the inner fountain. Fish here, get swallowed, find the Power Glove.
7. Find the southeast corner and exit.
8. Outside to the pool area and strangle the Root Devil.
9. Fish a Heart Container out of the pool.
10. Return to the gift shop and buy a telescope. If <10 gold, go outside and zoo some animals.
11. Telescope west from there to see the season blocks password.
12. North and east to the season blocks, push with Power Glove, forget the password, go look at it again, etc.
13. Follow halls around to the Sphinx. Solve her riddle with the Snowglobe.
14. Collect purse upgrade from the monk.

4:22

=== Ice Palace (Skipping Ahead) ===

1. Enter Cave of Cheating.
2. Get Broom, Candy, and Gold. Plus Compass, if you want to complete the Ice Palace's map.
3. Fly northwest to the Ice Palace. It's due north of Botire, or just west of Magnetic North.
4. Beat the Ice Dragon at six different games.

5:20

=== Cheat To The End ===

1. Enter Cave of Cheating.
2. Take Broom, Stick, Compass, Candy. (Stick, Compass, and Candy are only required for the Toll Troll). +Stopwatch to make the Rhinoceros easier if you want.
3. Strangle the meadow Root Devil.
4. Straight West, give Stick to the Toll Troll and Broom past the Rhinoceros. Strangle Root Devil.
5. North to the Temple. Fly up the escalator and strangle Root Devil.
6. Northeast to Ice Palace. From the hub, go southwest, then north, then down the ladder.
7. Fly east (don't bother with the minefield), past Moon Song go south, find the treadle and press it.
8. Back out of Ice Palace the way you came.
9. Northeast to Magnetic North, strangle Root Devil.
10. Fly to the East edge, middle latitude, trigger the kidnap cutscene.
11. Take Vanishing Cream from the Princess, then abandon her. Follow the tunnel to its door and leave.
12. Strangle Root Devil.
13. Just south of that Root Devil, enter the cave and press the treadle.
14. Around the corner southwest of there, strangle another Root Devil.
15. Enter the Cheapside house by the bus stop, push chair, downstairs go East.
16. Follow this tunnel all the way around the East edge (don't go up the first ladder).
17. Come out by the Fractia Root Devil; strangle him.

6:38

=== Run For Mayor ===

1. Get six coins via battle or zoo, and pick up a stick.
2. Buy Divining Rod.
3. Follow the top right root past the Toll Troll into Fractia.
4. Enter City Hall and run for Mayor.
5. Get five endorsements. I usually start with the Hospital and work around clockwise, stopping before the Food Service Guild.
6. Return to City Hall and hold the election.
7. Follow root to the Root Devil and strangle him.

7:40

=== Rescue the Princess (Skipping Ahead) ===

1. Enter Cave of Cheating.
2. Take Hookshot from the left guy. OPTIONAL: Take Bell too, and clear out the mountain and desert monsters first. OR: Broom instead of Hookshot, goes a bit faster.
3. From Cheapside, hook eastward and keep going east and a little north.
4. Get kidnapped.
5. Take Vanishing Cream from the Princess and use it.
6. Circle around to Skull Lake and Hook the Jail Key.
7. Go back to the Jail, open it (just touch), then escort the Princess out the front door.
8. Proceed all the way south, then west, to the sand castle.
9. Sell Princess to the King.

10:03
With Broom and Bell: 4:55
